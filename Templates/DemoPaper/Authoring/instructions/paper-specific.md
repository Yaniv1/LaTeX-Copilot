# Paper-Specific Instructions

## Demo Objective

Demonstrate a concise, rigorous critical reading of a scientific paper.
Identify the central claim, reconstruct the evidence chain, evaluate whether
the methodology supports the claim, and distinguish demonstrated limitations
from speculative criticism.

## Scope

Use a small illustrative structure rather than a full literature review.
Emphasize reproducibility, validity, assumptions, missing evidence, and
plausible alternative interpretations.

## Starting Point

Replace the placeholders in `Paper/main.tex` or register an existing draft
under `Authoring/intake/source/`.
