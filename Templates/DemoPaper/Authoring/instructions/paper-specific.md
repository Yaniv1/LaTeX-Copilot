# Paper-Specific Instructions

## Demo Objective

Demonstrate a concise, rigorous critical reading of a scientific paper.
Identify the central claim, reconstruct the evidence chain, evaluate whether
the methodology supports the claim, and distinguish demonstrated limitations
from speculative criticism.

## Scope

Use a small illustrative structure rather than a full literature review.
Emphasize reproducibility, validity, assumptions, missing evidence, and
plausible alternative interpretations.

## Section Instructions

### Introduction

Explain why critical reading requires reconstructing the paper's argument
before evaluating it. Establish the distinction between disagreement with a
topic and evidence against a reported study.

### Method

Describe a traceable review process for claims, evidence, assumptions,
validity threats, reproducibility artifacts, and alternative explanations.

### Discussion and Conclusion

Distinguish demonstrated defects from limitations that narrow generalization.
State what additional evidence or analysis would resolve each important
uncertainty.
